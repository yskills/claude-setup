# Audit of HQ, the setup repo and the process

54 agents: five auditors (HQ page, HQ data and flows, repo, outcomes, security and cost), one skeptic per high or medium finding. Only findings the skeptic confirmed are listed; refuted ones are at the end so nobody re-finds them.

**Grade: B.** The build engine works and the money numbers are honest. But HQ's "what needs you" view can't be trusted yet, and nothing has earned real money so far.

**What's good**
- **Money:** amounts are stored in cents and test money is labelled. finance/duo-test matches Stripe (28,80 € net). The money rules pass 40 of 40 tests in CI, and the published HQ page matches the template.
- **HQ page security:** no unsafe HTML insertion, links are https only, every CDN script is pinned with a hash, and there are no secrets in the repo or the db.
- **Building:** Kleingarten slices 1 to 3 merged at 5/5 in about 7 h. Every CI step passes locally.

**Where we're off**
1. **HQ shows an old snapshot as if it were current (medium).** projects/claude-setup.json still says "Setup done (PR 28)… Roblox on your go", but PRs 29 to 52 merged since. pm/now is all about Kleingarten, and the team/* rows are several PRs behind. Rows have no timestamps, and the "list is stale" warning can never show while a refresher session is set up (page line 1307).
2. **The "needs you" count is wrong both ways (medium).** The bell says 5 because an English summary line repeats the 4 German Kleingarten to-dos; Company says 4. Three real to-dos aren't in HQ at all: the "go" a thread is waiting on, deleting 27 old branches, and the PC setup steps.
3. **HQ updates depend on threads remembering (medium).** 14 merged PRs have no XP event, so HQ shows level 5 when it should be 6. gate.md has no step for HQ; only operator SKILL.md:139 mentions it.
4. **0 € of real money, and demand has never been tested (medium).** duo-test is paused and only has test money, Kleingarten is still private, and the test-3 demand probe row is empty (TEST-PROJECTS.md:43). 17 of the 25 setup PRs since HQ started went into HQ.
5. **Docs still describe a merge tap that no longer exists (medium).** "Merge tap" appears in 5 places (README:101, publish/SKILL.md:21, sell/SKILL.md:158, sell/keys.md:22, TEST-PROJECTS:10). There's no written way to undo a bad deploy or migration (Workers rollback, D1 Time Travel), and no automatic secret scan in cloud threads. None of this is live yet, but it matters from the first live Stripe key.
6. **HQ breaks our own UI rules (medium).**
   - **Language:** the greeting, the bottom tab bar and the Company, Plan and Team pages are in English.
   - **Contrast:** the small grey text is 3.46:1 against the background. White on the green buttons is 2.63:1, and 1.86:1 in dark mode.
   - **Taste notes:** your taste calls sit in designer.md, while TASTE.md (the file design-critic actually reads) is empty.

**What I'd do**
1. **Make HQ tell the truth** (about 2 h, I can do it alone):
   - Rewrite the stale rows now.
   - The gate's merge step writes the XP events and the project and team rows in one batch.
   - The daily refresh fills in missing PR events.

   - Each to-do lives in one place, in German.
2. **Docs and safety pass** (about 1 h, I can do it alone):
   - Remove the 5 "merge tap" lines.
   - Add one undo line per kind of change to gate.md.
   - Add a secret scan to new projects' checks.
   - Make check.mjs catch dead links and banned phrases like "merge tap".
3. **Run the demand probe (needs you):** one offer, one channel, 14 days, with the go number fixed before it starts. That's one thread plus your outreach. Your call: freeze HQ looks until then, bugs only.
4. **German and contrast pass on HQ** (about 1.5 h, I can do it alone): one German string table, darker grey text and green buttons, and keyboard focus that stays inside an open panel.
5. **Small stuff** (about 45 min, I can do it alone):
   - Move your taste calls into TASTE.md.
