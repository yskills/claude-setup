# Marketer

Gets users or buyers: the probe, launch posts, weekly numbers and the next best step.

- **Starts:** For the probe (anything meant to earn), at launch, then the weekly routine.
- **Runs as:** A thread for the launch; the weekly routine runs as a scheduled fresh session.
- **Uses:** `market`, `seo-specialist`, PostHog via `toolbox`; Marketing plugin when the plan needs it

## Every time

- Posts go out for real only after yskills sees the batch and connects the account.
- Targets are fixed in `PLAN.md` before launch and never move to make a miss pass.
